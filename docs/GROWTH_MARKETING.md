Neno Safari is live on Google Play as `com.nenosafari`.

Public listing: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nenosafari`

Ready-to-use posts and messages live in `docs/LAUNCH_KIT.md`. Weekly scorecards and channel tracking live in `docs/LAUNCH_TRACKING.md`.

## Launch Baseline

Store mirrors and launch posts show that the app is public, free, early in distribution, and still in the first-learning phase of growth. Treat the next 90 days as a focused launch sprint, not a broad advertising spend.

Current public signals to track:

- Play Store listing visits
- Install conversion rate
- Total installs and country split
- Rating and review count
- Crashes and ANRs
- Uninstalls after first install
- Search terms in Play Console

## Positioning

Core promise:

Learn useful Swahili words through quick Tanzania-inspired puzzles, then strengthen them with short review sprints.

Short pitch:

Neno Safari is a free Swahili vocabulary game inspired by Tanzania. Find hidden Kiswahili words, hear pronunciation, save useful vocabulary, and strengthen memory through focused review sprints.

Why people should care:

- It is specific to Swahili and Tanzanian culture.
- It feels lighter than a formal language lesson.
- It works well for beginners, students, tourists, diaspora families, and casual puzzle players.
- It is free, offline-friendly, and has no ads in the current release.
- Its saved-word review system gives learners a concrete reason to return without requiring an account.

## Best Early Audiences

- Swahili learners searching for easy practice
- Tanzanians who enjoy casual mobile word games
- Parents and teachers looking for vocabulary practice
- Tourists and volunteers preparing for Tanzania travel
- Diaspora families who want children to see Kiswahili as playful
- African tech, edtech, and indie game communities

## First 100 Users

Do these before paid ads:

1. Post the launch from the founder account with a direct Google Play link and 3 screenshots.
2. Ask every friend, tester, and supporter to download, play one puzzle, and send one honest note.
3. Ask happy players for a Play Store review after they have played, outside the app.
4. Share in Swahili learning, Tanzania travel, African tech, indie game, and education groups where self-promotion is allowed.
5. DM 20 Swahili teachers, Tanzania travel creators, and education creators with a short personal note and the Play link.
6. Turn feedback into a visible "new this week" update.

Use the copy blocks in `docs/LAUNCH_KIT.md` so this can start immediately without rewriting posts from scratch.

## Organic Channels

### LinkedIn

Best for founder credibility, African tech, edtech, and professional networks.

Post ideas:

- Launch story: why the game exists.
- Build story: screenshots from idea to Play Store.
- Culture story: one Tanzanian word and the story behind it.
- Progress story: "first 100 downloads" and what players asked for.

### TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts

Best for discovery. Keep videos under 20 seconds.

Formats:

- "Can you find SIMBA before I do?"
- "Swahili word of the day: safari"
- "3 words to know before visiting Tanzania"
- Screen recording of one quick puzzle win
- Before/after: boring vocab list vs Neno Safari puzzle

### WhatsApp And Telegram

Best for the first trusted wave.

Use a short message:

```text
Neno Safari is live on Google Play. It is a free Swahili word-search game inspired by Tanzania. Please try one puzzle and tell me what word/category we should add next:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nenosafari
```

### Reddit / Facebook Groups

Use carefully and follow group rules. Focus on useful posts, not drive-by promotion.

Good post angle:

"I built a free Swahili word-search game for beginners. I would love feedback from learners and native speakers on the vocabulary examples."

## Paid Ads

Do not spend heavily until the store page converts.

Start with the Google Ads test in `docs/CHEAP_AD_TEST_PLAN.md`, because the app is Android-first and the campaign can point directly at the Google Play listing.

Stop ads if:

- Install cost is high and no one reviews or shares.
- Store listing visits do not convert.
- Reviews mention confusion that screenshots or onboarding can fix.

Scale ads only after:

- Store conversion is healthy.
- At least 10-20 real reviews exist.
- Screenshots and description clearly show the game.
- Crashes and ANRs are clean.

Keep TikTok and Reddit paid tests for later unless there is extra budget. Their official minimums/recommendations are too high for a cautious first learning test.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

Monday:

- Check Play Console installs, reviews, crashes, and search terms.
- Pick one feedback theme to act on.

Tuesday / Wednesday:

- Ship one small content, copy, screenshot, or retention improvement.

Thursday:

- Post one gameplay clip and one word-of-the-day post.

Friday:

- Message 5-10 creators, teachers, or community leads.

Weekend:

- Ask players a simple question and collect category requests.

## 30-Day Targets

- 100+ real installs
- 10+ honest Play Store reviews
- 5+ direct feedback conversations
- 2 short gameplay videos posted each week
- 1 store screenshot refresh if current screenshots do not sell the game clearly
- 1 small live update based on player feedback

## 90-Day Targets

- 500-1,000 installs
- 30+ reviews with a strong average rating
- One repeatable channel that drives installs
- One teacher/creator/community partnership
- One visible retention update, such as Weekly Safari Challenge or stronger daily rewards

## What Not To Do Yet

- Do not add ads or in-app purchases before trust and retention are stronger.
- Do not spend heavily on paid ads before store conversion is known.
- Do not add analytics until privacy policy and Data Safety answers are updated.
- Do not chase many categories before the current content feels deep and polished.
