- Decide whether the store page is ready for a tiny paid test.

## Tiny Paid Ad Test

Only run after the first organic push has produced some installs and feedback.

Start:

- Budget: USD 5-10/day.
- Duration: 7 days.
- Goal: installs.
- Creative: real gameplay video or screenshot carousel.
- Countries: Tanzania, Kenya, United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Germany.

Stop if:

- People click but do not install.
- Reviews say the app expectation does not match the listing.
- Crashes or ANRs appear in Play Console.

Scale only after:

- Store conversion is healthy.
- Reviews are positive.
- Screenshots clearly show the actual game.
