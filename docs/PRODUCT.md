# Product Notes

> Current product baseline: Neno Safari 2.13.0, prepared as Android build 38.

## Concept

Neno Safari helps players learn Tanzanian Swahili vocabulary through word search puzzles. Each puzzle is based on a familiar Tanzanian theme such as food, cities, animals, transport, markets, weather, culture, and national life.

The game should feel proudly Tanzanian, friendly, useful, and polished without feeling childish.

## Live Product Posture

Neno Safari is now live on Google Play. Product decisions should optimize for repeat play, trust, and vocabulary quality rather than simply increasing the size of the app.

The strongest next move is more depth in the current categories, Daily Puzzle, and Word of the Day pools before adding many new categories. Players are more likely to return for fresh daily content, visible progress, and mastery goals than for a longer category list.

Public product link: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nenosafari`

## Target Audience

- Beginner and intermediate Swahili learners
- Tanzanians who want a casual vocabulary game
- Tourists learning common Swahili words
- Students and kids practicing vocabulary
- People interested in Tanzanian culture

## Launch Positioning

One-line promise:

Learn useful Swahili words through quick Tanzania-inspired word search puzzles.

Best early wedge:

Neno Safari is not trying to outspend large language apps. It should win with a specific identity: Swahili-first, Tanzania-rooted, offline-friendly, light enough for casual play, and useful for beginners, students, travelers, and diaspora families.

First growth target:

Get the first 100-500 real players from warm communities, Swahili learning groups, Tanzanian tech/education networks, schools, travel creators, and friends of current players before scaling paid ads.

## Core Loop

1. Player chooses a mode.
2. Player chooses a category.
3. Game generates a letter grid and hides Swahili words.
4. Player taps or drags across letters.
5. Correct words are highlighted and checked off.
6. Learn mode shows the full learning card; faster modes show a compact Quick Learn card with optional details and pronunciation.
7. Player completes puzzle and earns score/stars.
8. Player saves useful words from Quick Learn or the full learning card.
9. Due saved words return in a short review sprint using Again, Hard, and Got It ratings.
10. Clearing the due queue updates memory strength and can award one daily review reward.

## Current Modes

- Classic: complete a themed puzzle without time pressure.
- Learn: emphasizes the word learning card.
- Timed: find words before time runs out.
- Daily: opens a daily category puzzle.
- Weekly Challenge: rotates themed vocabulary and grants a one-time weekly reward.

## Content Expansion Strategy

Priority order:

1. Expand Daily Puzzle and Word of the Day variety.
2. Maintain pronunciation and examples across all 432 puzzle and Word-of-Day entries.
3. Rotate Daily and Weekly themes using current content.
4. Add new categories only when player feedback proves a distinct learning need.

Current expansion rule:

- Deepen an existing category before adding another menu item.
- Require a reviewed vocabulary set, examples, pronunciation, daily/weekly reuse plan, and mobile grid-fit check before launch.

## UX Goals

- Easy to start in one or two taps.
- Clear word list and readable grid on phones.
- Swahili-first interface with optional English support.
- Encouraging feedback: "Hongera!", "Umefanikiwa!", "Endelea hivyo!"
- Learning moments should be short, useful, and skippable.
- Returning players should see a clear reason to play today.
- Progress, streaks, saved-word review, and badges should quietly pull players into one more puzzle.
- Review scheduling must remain honest: future words stay out of the due queue, and mastery only changes after a recall rating.

## Monetization Ideas For Later

Keep the MVP learning-focused first. Possible future options:

- Optional ad-supported hints
- Premium offline puzzle packs
- Audio pronunciation pack
- School/classroom package
- Cosmetic themes inspired by Tanzania
