Neno Safari Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 6, 2026

Made by NECUVA GROUP LIMITED

Neno Safari is a Swahili vocabulary word search game made by NECUVA GROUP LIMITED. This policy explains how the app handles information for Android beta testing, the PWA, and future store releases.

Summary

Neno Safari is designed to work offline and does not require an account. The app does not collect personal information in the current release.

Data Stored On Device

The app stores game progress locally on the player's device using app/browser storage:

  • Total score
  • Words found
  • Puzzles completed
  • Category progress
  • Saved words
  • Daily puzzle streak
  • Coins, badges, and owned cosmetic items
  • Settings such as language, sound, motion, and contrast preferences

This information stays on the device and is used only to run the game experience.

Data We Do Not Collect

Neno Safari does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Payment information
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Analytics events
  • Advertising ID

Audio

The pronunciation button can use local audio files included with the app or the device/browser speech synthesis feature. Neno Safari does not record the player's voice and does not send player audio to any service.

Audio generation API keys are kept on the developer machine and are not bundled into the app.

Children

The game is educational and may be used by children. Neno Safari does not include accounts, chat, public profiles, direct messaging, ads, or in-app purchases in the current release.

Third Parties

The current app does not share player data with third-party analytics, advertising, or social platforms.

Future Changes

If analytics, cloud saves, ads, purchases, accounts, or online features are added later, this policy will be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact NECUVA GROUP LIMITED using the developer contact details shown on the Google Play Store listing.
